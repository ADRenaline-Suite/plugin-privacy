A privacy note that lists every possible data type.

## Steps

1. Name the activity in the person's words. List only categories they named.
2. The purpose is why this activity exists, in their words. Do not add a purpose to look complete.
3. Legal basis is a heading left as a gap unless a qualified person already wrote it. Do not fill it.

## Rules

1. Legal basis stays a gap when nobody qualified wrote it.
2. Do not write an identifier that singles out a person. A name, a record number, or a free-text note that identifies someone stays out. An aggregate figure is the fact.
